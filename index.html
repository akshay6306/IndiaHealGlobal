Nutritional Science

Ayurvedic Dosha-Specific Diets & Sattvic Nutrition

"One man's food is another man's poison." Ayurvedic nutrition is customized to your body constitution (Prakriti).

💨 Air & Space

Vata-Pacifying Diet

Profile: Lean, creative, energetic. Imbalance causes anxiety, dry skin & digestive sensitivity.

Goal: Grounding, warming, moisturizing.

✅ Eat: Warm cooked foods, brown rice, root veggies, bananas, ghee & warm milk.
❌ Avoid: Cold raw salads, crackers, dry snacks, excess caffeine.
🔥 Fire & Water

Pitta-Pacifying Diet

Profile: Muscular, motivated. Imbalance causes anger, acid reflux, inflammation & overheating.

Goal: Cooling, soothing, moderately dense.

✅ Eat: Cucumber, melons, leafy greens, coconut water, oats.
❌ Avoid: Spicy, acidic, fermented foods, vinegar, hot peppers, red meat.
🌊 Earth & Water

Kapha-Pacifying Diet

Profile: Sturdy, calm. Imbalance causes lethargy, weight gain & sinus congestion.

Goal: Lightening, warming, stimulating.

✅ Eat: Steamed veggies, legumes, apples, ginger, black pepper, turmeric.
❌ Avoid: Heavy oily foods, cold dairy, dense sweets, heavy oils.
✨ Meditative State

The Sattvic Diet

Profile: Prescribed in traditional ashrams for spiritual healing and mental clarity.

Goal: Promoting absolute mental peace and lightness.

✅ Eat: Fresh fruits, whole grains, raw nuts, seeds, fresh cow's milk & herbal teas.
❌ Avoid: Stale food, onions, garlic, processed foods, alcohol.

Official e-Visa & e-FRRO Guide

How to Apply & Extend Your Medical Visa in India

India offers 1-year e-Medical Visas (e-MV) for 160+ countries. Extensions are processed 100% online through e-FRRO.

🔄 Need to Extend Your Current Indian Medical Visa?

Must be applied 15 to 30 days before expiration on the official government e-FRRO portal. Overstaying incurs severe financial penalties.

👉 Extend Your Visa (e-FRRO Portal) →
Step 1
📋

Obtain Hospital "Treatment Continuation" Documents

Your hospital's International Patient Desk must issue:

📄 Medical Certificate: Signed by treating doctor detailing diagnosis & extension necessity
🏥 Hospital Recommendation Letter: Official request on letterhead
📊 Discharge/Treatment Summary: Showing timeline of ongoing care
Step 2
📁

Prepare Scanned Personal Documents

Passport (valid for 6+ months beyond new requested stay)
📸 Passport photo with white background
🏨 Proof of Residence (Form C): Provided by hospital, hotel, or serviced apartment
💳 Proof of Funds / Bank statements
Step 3
💻

Submit Application on e-FRRO Portal

Visit indianfrro.gov.in, register with an email & Indian mobile number, select "Visa Extension", and upload scanned files.

Step 4
⏳

Pay Fee & Receive Extension Certificate

Pay online after document review. Processing takes 3 to 15 working days. The extension certificate is emailed directly to you.
