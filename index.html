Official Guide

How to Apply for an India Medical Visa

India offers a dedicated e-Medical Visa (e-MV) valid for 1 year with up to 3 entries. Available for citizens of 160+ countries. Processing: 3–5 business days. We assist you at every step.

🛂

e-Medical Visa (e-MV)

For patients seeking medical treatment at recognised Indian hospitals. Valid 1 year, triple entry.

🌿

e-Medical Attendant Visa

For up to 2 family members or caregivers accompanying the patient. Same validity as patient's visa.

🧘

Ayush Visa (2023)

Introduced in 2023 for traditional medicine & wellness travel (Ayurveda, Yoga, Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Homeopathy).

Step 1
📋

Check Eligibility & Gather Documents

Confirm your country is eligible for e-Medical Visa at indianvisaonline.gov.in. Prepare the required documents:

🛂 Valid passport (6+ months validity, 2 blank pages)
📸 Recent passport-size photograph (white background)
📄 Medical documents: diagnosis report, doctor's referral letter
🏥 Invitation/appointment letter from an Indian hospital
💳 Credit/debit card for fee payment
💡 We provide: IndiaHeal Global obtains the hospital invitation letter on your behalf — for free.
Step 2
💻

Apply Online at indianvisaonline.gov.in

Visit the official Government of India visa portal and complete the online application form:

🌐 Go to: indianvisaonline.gov.in
✅ Select: "Medical Visa" as the visa category
📝 Fill in all personal, travel & medical details
📤 Upload: passport scan, photograph, medical documents
💳 Pay the visa fee online (varies by country)
⚠️ Important: Only apply on the official Government of India portal. Avoid third-party websites charging extra fees.
Step 3
💰

Visa Fees & Processing Time

Country GroupFee (approx.)Processing
🇺🇸 USA$80 USD3–5 working days
🇬🇧 UK£80 GBP3–5 working days
🇦🇺 AustraliaAUD 1103–5 working days

📌 Fees are approximate and subject to change. Verify on the official portal at time of application.
Step 4
📧

Receive Your e-Visa & Travel to India

Upon approval, you receive your Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) by email. Print it and carry with your passport.

✅ Print the e-Visa ETA document (2 copies recommended)
🛫 Enter through designated e-Visa airports: Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Kolkata and more
🧳 At immigration: present passport + printed e-Visa + medical appointment letter
🚗 Our coordinator meets you at arrivals — no need to worry about transport
🎁 Free service: We escort you from arrivals through immigration and to your hospital/hotel. Zero stress.
Step 5
🔄

Visa Extension (if needed)

If your treatment requires a longer stay than your visa validity, you can apply for an extension in India:

🏛️ Apply at the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO) in your city
📄 Submit: hospital letter recommending extension + medical progress reports
🌐 Online FRRO: indianfrro.gov.in
⏱️ Extension granted in 7–10 working days usually
✅ We assist: IndiaHeal Global's team handles all FRRO paperwork and appointments on your behalf.
